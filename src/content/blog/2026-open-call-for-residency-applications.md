The Museum of Play is now accepting applications for its 2027 residency cohort. Each year, we welcome artists, designers, and makers into our studios for a season of focused work, with full access to the Museum's collections and the guidance of our curatorial team.

## What residents receive

- A private studio in our East Wing for the length of the residency
- Access to the collections, including objects not currently on view
- A stipend for materials and living expenses
- One-on-one mentorship with a member of our curatorial staff
- A culminating public showing in the Museum's galleries

## Who should apply

We are looking for practitioners in any medium whose work engages play, games, toys, or the culture around them. Early-career and established artists are equally encouraged to apply, and no prior affiliation with the Museum is required.

## How to apply

Applications are open from September through November. Applicants will be asked to submit a short statement describing their proposed project, a portfolio of recent work, and two references. Finalists will be invited to a short virtual interview in the new year.
