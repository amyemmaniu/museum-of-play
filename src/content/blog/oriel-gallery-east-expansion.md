The Museum of Play is growing. Thanks to the generosity of our members and donors, we are building the Oriel Gallery East, a new wing that will add more than 8,000 square feet of exhibition space to the Museum.

## What's coming

The expansion will house a large, flexible gallery for rotating exhibitions, a dedicated education studio for school groups and workshops, and a light-filled corridor that connects the new wing to the existing galleries. Tall windows and an oriel-style bay give the space its name and fill it with natural light. The new gallery will sit opposing the Oriel Gallery West.

## Why it matters

Our collection has outgrown our walls. Only a small share of it is on view at any given time, and the new gallery will let us show more of it, more often. The additional space will also allow us to host larger traveling exhibitions and to give contemporary artists room to make ambitious, site-specific work.

## How you can help

Construction is underway, and the campaign is still open to new supporters. Gifts of every size help fund the build, the gallery's opening exhibition, and free admission days for local families. To learn more, visit our [support page](/support).
