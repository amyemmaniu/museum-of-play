Come hungry! The Museum of Play is thrilled to welcome Sable Sparrow, our new café on the first floor. It's the perfect spot to grab some grub before, during, or after your visit.

## What's on the menu

Sable Sparrow serves breakfast and lunch all day, with a rotating selection of soups, sandwiches, salads, and pastries made with ingredients from local suppliers. Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options are always available, and the kids' menu features small portions of café favorites.

Coffee, tea, and seasonal drinks are served from the counter. A rotating menu of specials inspired by what's on currently [on view](/on-view) is available too!

## Where to find us

Sable Sparrow is located on the first floor, next to the entrance to the Sculpture Corridor. Tables are arranged in a bright, open seating area, with communal tables for groups and quieter spots by the windows.

## Good to know

- Open during regular Museum hours; no admission ticket is needed to visit the café
- [Members](/membership) receive a discount on food and drinks
- The café is fully accessible. High chairs are available upon request

We can't wait to see you at the table!
