## Description

What makes a house a home? *This House is Not a Home* is a lecture series that examines belonging through the lens of play, from dollhouses and tabletop role-playing games to the games children invent in the spaces they're given.

Each session pairs a guest speaker with a short object study from the Museum's collection. Topics include miniature houses as portraits of domestic life, the role of play in shaping children's sense of safety, and what it means to build a home in a game when you don't have one at all.

Talks run about 45 minutes, followed by a moderated discussion and a reception in the atrium. Sessions are free with Museum admission, and seating is first come, first served.

## How to attend

- Doors open 30 minutes before each talk
- ASL interpretation and live captioning are available; request them when you register
- Recordings will be posted on our blog after each session
