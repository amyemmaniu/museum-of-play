## Description

Look up. Chloë Braun's permanent installation invites visitors to do exactly that, and to keep looking.

The series of large-format black and white photographs captures the ceilings, domes, and skylights of buildings built for play, from old movie palaces to arcades and community halls. Shot from directly below, each image flattens the architecture into pure geometry: concentric rings, radiating ribs, and a single circle of light at the center.

Hung low and arranged in a gentle spiral, the photographs encourage visitors to lie back on the gallery's benches and see the rooms the way a child on the floor might. Braun reminds us that the spaces we play in shape how we feel about play itself.

## How to visit

- Benches and floor cushions are provided; feel free to recline
- Large-print labels and a descriptive audio guide are available at the entrance
- The gallery is step-free and fully accessible
