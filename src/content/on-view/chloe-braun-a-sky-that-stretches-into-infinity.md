## Description

Look up. Chloë Braun's custom-built skyspace invites visitors to do exactly that, and to keep looking.

Braun takes inspiration from the textures of 35mm film and the classic shapes of tangram puzzles when placing panes of grisaille stained glass to create intricate geometric designs. The artist asks how the objects we choose to preserve can act as windows to not only the past, but statements of what we treasure and the narratives we write for future generations.

## How to visit

- Benches are available around the perimeter of the skyspace
- The installation has been designated as a Sensory-Friendly Space, meaning its light and sound levels are suitable for those with [sensory needs](/plan-your-visit)
