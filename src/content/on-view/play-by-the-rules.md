## Description

Board games are never just games. They carry the values, anxieties, and politics of the people who made them and the eras that contextualize them. *Play by the Rules* draws on the Museum's collection of more than 300 board and card games to show how play has reflected, and sometimes challenged, the world around it.

Explore early 20th-century games that taught children about commerce and empire, wartime games that turned current events into entertainment, and modern designs that ask players to cooperate, negotiate, and organize. Along the way, you'll see how a game's rules, its box art, and even its playing pieces tell a story about who it was made for and who was left out.

The exhibition closes with a table of playable games, where visitors can try their hand at both classics and contemporary designs.
