# Description

Every year, the Museum hands a handful of students a simple but demanding assignment: pick a question about play and chase it wherever it leads. This exhibit gathers what they found.

Rather than polished answers, you'll find the research process on view: sketchbooks, annotated photographs, abandoned drafts, and the occasional prototype that didn't work. Look for the margin notes and crossed-out ideas. They show how a hunch becomes an argument, and how an object in a drawer becomes a story.

The work is arranged by question, not by student, so you may notice ideas echoing across projects. A fold in a paper toy, the wear on a game board, and the curve of a slide can all point to the same truth: people have always shaped play, and play has always shaped them.

## How to visit

- Start anywhere. There is no required path.
- Look for the "?" cards throughout the space and leave a question of your own.
- Seating, large-print labels, and a descriptive audio guide are available at the entrance.

*The Sandbox Scholars Fellowship is supported by the Museum's education and curatorial programs.*
